## Suggested checkpoints

After chapter 5, explain why a low matching-row percentage need not imply low page traffic. After chapter 9, distinguish an index predicate from an arbitrary application assumption. After chapter 12, distinguish covering values from heap visibility. After chapter 16, defend the write cost of a read optimization. After chapter 19, reproduce a concurrency anomaly instead of reasoning from a sequential demonstration. After chapter 21, explain what partitioning improves and what it does not.
